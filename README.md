# 🌍 Global Superstore Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze the **Global Superstore dataset**, offering insights into sales performance, profit distribution, customer segments, and shipping efficiency across different regions and categories.

The dashboard helps users identify key sales trends, profitable segments, and regional performance patterns through visually engaging and dynamic charts.

---

## 🧠 Objectives
- Analyze overall **sales and profit performance** across regions and categories.
- Identify the **most profitable sub-categories** and **shipping modes**.
- Compare **payment modes and customer segments** based on sales contribution.
- Enable **interactive filtering** by Country, State, City, and Category for deeper insights.

---

## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – for data visualization and dashboard design  
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – for dataset preprocessing  
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – for calculated measures and KPIs  
- **Power Query Editor** – for data cleaning and transformation  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- The **West region** generated the highest sales (33.37%) followed by the **Central** and **South** regions.  
- **Consumer segment** contributed the maximum sales (~0.75M).  
- **COD payment mode** was the most preferred, generating around **0.67M** in sales.  
- **Standard shipping** accounted for the majority of sales (~0.91M).  
- **Phones and Chairs** were among the top-selling sub-categories, while **Tables and Supplies** showed lower profit margins.  

---

## 🎨 Dashboard Highlights
- Modern **dark theme** design with vibrant accents for better contrast and readability.  
- **Interactive filters**: Country, State, City, and Category.  
- **Variety of visuals**: Bar charts, Pie charts, Combo charts, and Cards.  
- **Responsive layout** suitable for both desktop and widescreen displays.
